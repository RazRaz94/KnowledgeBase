NG Dante changes since tutorial

↩ Return to DMC2 Notes List

Overview

The Overall Change stems from upgrading gun which scales Majin form with 2 most important moves "Doomsday" and Laser by 20%. Skip Rebellion Lv2 and buy Shotgun Lv2. Need at least 15k Orb gain during M7 and some other key A / S ranks from early and late game.

New Buy List

This is considering the perfect route.

  • M5: Shotgun Lv2, Purple Orb = 1,500 Orbs
  • M8: Vendetta Lv2, Purple Orb 2, 2x Devil Star L, 2x Devil Star S = 28,000 Orbs
  • M14: Purple Orb 3, 2x Devil Star S = 27,000 Orbs
  • M17: 2x Devil Star L = 24,000 Orbs

Changes

  • M2: Majin from Aerial Heart Door. Speeds up Goat fight by significant amount with doomsday.
  • M3: Quick Laser Boss.
  • M5: With Lv2 Shotgun it makes the dog fight more consistent also encourages the use of doomsday to speed up the tank fight. The ease and speed of setting up Red Hp for Majin ready chopper post-flight however is RNG.
  • M6: Demon Prix strategy since you can't do the old one anymore due to the lack of Reb Lv2.
  • M7: Early 2x E, D rank setup and few movement change.
  • M8: One RT then High Time DT transform to Spam RT till it runs out for helm breaker (For S Rank Finisher). Note that it needs some improvising on movement if the boss doesn't behave well.
  • M9: Luck dependant A rank strategy for flame bat near miss dodge and few movement change.
  • M10: Large Devil Star usage to RT both golem and DT shot the rest. Moth is basically same as Lucia strat. However, Dante has doomsday setup against larvas to end the fight very fast and pervent the first hatch.
  • M11: Possible S rank if you no damage bolverk with S rank style finish.
  • M12: Kick cancel the geometries. Boss strategy is same as M8 other than adding one regular hit before high time.
  • M13: RT Arius. Super RNG
  • M14:Pure RT spheres to make up Majin against Phantom because, Lv2 shotgun can help you reliably to finish him off fast. 5 Fire balls into laser but, this fight can go into south real quick if phantom pattern starts with covered face walking.
  • M15: HDC 2012 version Skip.
  • M16: Majin Blood Goat Fight. Early laser for both bolverk and trismagia (again thanks for Lv2 shotgun). Stuby roll for the sacrilege pick. Although, if the route was perfect with 1 S Devil Star you can Majin run from here all the way to bolverk to manipulate max laser damage. If you get super lucky and bolverk drops enough white orbs to Majin run to the elevator before it runs out.
  • M17: RT Arius. Super RNG
  • M18: New Argosax setup thanks to Lv2 Shotgun. 2x part active damage by Fire Ball Joka + Bull (Can be inconsistent and Lock-on can mess it up thanks to phantom) > L Devil Star before the laser comes out to damage both building and griffon > 7 Fireball against monkey + phantom to leave 1 part alive for despair setup. Early Majin laser for Despair is rare RNG.
